# Sample pages

Two OmniParseBench pages for `examples/quickstart.ipynb`:

- `scan.png`: a scanned Korean letter (OmniParseBench `multiling_finepdfs-images_kor_Hang_07603024-p1`), from [FinePDFs](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/finepdfs), ODC-BY 1.0.
- `equations.png`: an arXiv page with math (OmniParseBench `olmo_arxiv_math-2502.15977_pg21-p1`), from [olmOCR-bench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/olmOCR-bench), ODC-BY 1.0, rendered at 200 dpi.

Their full source and license notes are in OmniParseBench's [`licenses/`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/datalab-to/omni_parse_bench/tree/main/licenses) folder.
